For traders

A direct fee arrangement for experienced traders

For experienced, higher-volume traders who prefer a simple, direct arrangement to a membership. You register under a DA Affiliate link as an affiliate account and trade as usual.

01

Register under a DA Affiliate link

Open your account through a DA Affiliate link on one of the exchanges available now, set up as an affiliate account.

02

Fixed commission on your own fees

You receive a fixed commission on your own eligible trading fees, under exchange-specific terms. Terms are confirmed per exchange; not every exchange supports identical terms.

03

Credited by the exchange

Where the exchange supports it, the commission is credited through the exchange's own affiliate system.

04

Signal-only channel

You get access to a signal-only channel. Every trading decision stays your own.

This is not the DA Cashback membership. The daily plan, member community, learning and support are part of DA Cashback. See DA Cashback (opens in a new tab)

Build your channel with DA behind you

01

Direct referral business

Refer traders to the exchanges available now through your DA Affiliate link. Commission comes from the trading fees of traders you refer directly.

02

Exchange partnership arrangements

How it works

How affiliate commission works

Exchanges share part of the trading fees they collect with the affiliate account that referred the trader. Each exchange sets its own terms.

01

Link

Your referral link

A trader registers on an exchange through your link and is tied to your affiliate account.

02

Fees

A share of trading fees

When that trader pays trading fees, the exchange pays a share to the affiliate account. The share depends on the exchange, the product and the programme's terms.

03

1level

Direct referrals only

Commission comes only from traders you refer directly. Terms can change when an exchange updates its programme.

Rates and reward tiers may change according to each exchange's policy and partner program.

Reconciliation

How DA reconciles commissions

Before a commission figure is published, it is checked against the exchanges' own records.

  1. 01

    Exchange records

    Commission data starts from each exchange's affiliate dashboard for accounts registered under DA Affiliate links.

  2. 02

    Received versus paid

    For each period, DA matches commission received from exchanges against commission paid to partners. Pending or cancelled amounts are left out.

  3. 03

    Consent before publication

    Partner stories, quotes and screenshots appear only with the partner's written consent.
